This private development candidate requires macOS 14.4 or later (Apple silicon).

ENG Drawing Viewer for Mac (Apple silicon)
==========================================

Install
-------
Drag "ENG Drawing Viewer" onto the Applications folder.

Open it the first time
----------------------
ENG Drawing Viewer is free software from an independent developer. It is not
notarized by Apple, so the first time you open it macOS shows a warning.

1. Open the app from Applications. When macOS says it could not verify the app,
   click Done.
2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click
   Open Anyway next to the message about ENG Drawing Viewer.
3. Confirm with your password or Touch ID, then click Open.

You only do this once. Please download the app only from
https://ca-works.github.io/eng-drw/ and check the SHA-256 checksum listed there.

Privacy
-------
Everything runs on your Mac. The app has no account, no telemetry and makes no
network connections. Your PDFs are opened read-only and are never changed.
